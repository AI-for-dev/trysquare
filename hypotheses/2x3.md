# Hypothesis: a project rule against a well written ticket

Declared before measuring. A hypothesis written afterwards is a conclusion wearing
a disguise, and the point of writing it down is to make a disappointing result
publishable instead of quietly reframed.

## What is predicted

Both bricks reduce ticket overflow relative to the baseline, and **the well written
ticket does more than the project rule**. The reasoning budget is a witness: it does
not change what is in the context window, so if it moved the criterion on its own,
the gain would not be attributable to context at all.

## What would falsify it

- The careful ticket does not separate from the baseline. Then a well specified task
  is not the lever the course claims, and the module's central claim needs rewriting
  rather than rephrasing.
- The rule alone establishes as strongly as the ticket. Then filing a convention in
  a file is enough, and the effort belongs there rather than in ticket writing.
- The `nothing / high` witness moves the criterion as much as either brick. Then
  what is being measured is reasoning effort, not context, and every conclusion
  drawn from this matrix about context is unsupported.
- Any cell reaches 0/n **and** the baseline also reaches 0/n. Then the criterion is
  saturated and the matrix discriminates nothing.

## What is not claimed

Nothing about cost. Tokens, turns and duration are reported, but they are
contaminated by retries whenever the provider cuts the stream, and a retry replays
the whole accumulated context. A cost column is only readable when retries are near
zero across the cells being compared.

Nothing about interaction unless it is resampled directly. Two effects can both be
established without their difference being established, so "the rule pays more when
the model reasons" needs the difference of differences, not two intervals compared
by eye.

## The published result, for the record

Measured at n=10, 60 runs, zero retries:

|                   | thinking off | thinking high |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ------------- |
| nothing           | 10/10        | 7/10          |
| project rule      | 9/10         | 2/10          |
| careful ticket    | 0/10         | 0/10          |

Established: the careful ticket at -100 points with complete separation, and the
rule **with** reasoning at -80 points [-100, -50]. The rule alone is inconclusive at
-10 points.

So the prediction held for the ticket and only half held for the rule: a convention
filed in a file does nothing unless the reasoning that goes and reads it is paid
for. Three reservations travel with it - the interaction is not established, the
witnesses do not leave the same room (10/10 against 7/10, so part of the apparent
interaction is mechanical), and 0 out of 10 does not mean never: the rule of three
bounds the true rate at roughly 30%, which is exactly why a mechanism is needed
downstream.

## Smoke passes

A run at n=2 is **not** a test of this hypothesis and no conclusion may be drawn
from it. It exists to check that the harness is wired: that every run is valid, that
the outputs are complete, and that the thinking level a cell declares is the level
that actually ran.
